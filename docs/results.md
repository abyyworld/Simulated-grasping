## Ablation: rotation augmentation

Two runs with identical data, architecture, schedule and seed; the only difference is whether training images are randomly rotated (with the grasp label carried along).

| | no rotation | with rotation |
|---|---|---|
| best validation AP | 0.807 | 0.822 |
| best epoch | 11 | 10 |
| mean grasp-angle error, elongated objects | 48° | **47°** |
| per-angle quality spread at the chosen pixel | 0.032 | **0.026** |

Validation AP measures *where* to grasp and is barely affected. The angle metrics are the point: without augmentation each episode supervises one of twelve angle bins at one pixel, so the network predicts the angle-marginal success rate and orientation collapses. Random guessing over a half-turn-symmetric grasp scores 45°.
